Colorful (Simplified) ES-DE test adapter for Orbit

Original theme: Anthony Caccese
Original Colorful artwork: viking and faeran; additional contributors credited in README.md.
Source: https://github.com/anthonycaccese/colorful-simplified-es-de
License: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 2.0
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/2.0/

The original theme XML, artwork and fonts are preserved in this directory.
Orbit's native adaptation supports all nine bundled game-list variants,
the seven built-in color schemes, Medium/Large fonts and the 16:9 layout.
Metadata uses locally scraped game information; miximages can be composed
from locally downloaded images. Missing media falls back to available artwork.
ES-DE animations, audio, video playback, external add-ons and general theme
importing are not implemented.
The theme adaptations in EsDeTheme.kt and ColorfulSystemsView.kt are shared
under the same CC-BY-NC-SA 2.0 terms. Orbit's navigation bar is retained.
